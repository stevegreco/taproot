Add `<ds-slider>`, a form-associated slider for choosing one number from a range. It shows the current value beside the label, steps with the arrow, Page Up, Page Down, Home, and End keys, fires `input` while dragging and `change` once on release, and submits its value under `name`. `taproot get slider --impl web` lists its attributes and events.
