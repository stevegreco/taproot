Border lab

Make every edge earn its contrast.

Compare functional boundaries across foundation surfaces, then inspect focus, disabled, brand, and status intent.

Functional borders target at least 3:1 against every supported surface. Subtle and muted-brand borders are explicitly decorative and cannot be the only visual cue.
